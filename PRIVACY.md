# Privacy policy

This is a personal fork of NuvioTV. It is not a commercial service.

## Google Drive backup

The optional Google Drive backup uses the `drive.file` scope. The app can only see and change the file it creates itself (`nuvio-tv-backup.json`). It cannot read any other file in your Drive.

The backup file holds your IPTV source list and the fork's app settings. It is stored only in your own Google Drive. The developer does not receive, collect or store it.

The Google sign-in token stays on your device. You can remove it with "Sign out" in the app, or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Other data

The app does not send analytics or personal data to the developer.
